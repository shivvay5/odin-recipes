Samosa

Description

Samosas are flaky, fried or baked pastries filled with spiced potatoes, peas, and aromatics, popular across South Asia as a savory snack

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Salt
  • oil
  • Boiler Potatoes
  • Red Chilli powder
  • Masalas
  • Amchur

Steps

  1. Mix flour, salt, and oil; add water to form a firm dough, rest for 20 minutes.
  2. Roll dough into circles, cut in half, and shape into cones; fill with 1–2 tablespoons of filling.
  3. Seal the edges with water, ensuring no leaks, and shape into triangles.
  4. Deep-fry in medium-hot oil until golden and crisp, about 8–10 minutes.
  5. Your Samosas are Ready to eat

Tea

Description

Tea is a hot beverage made by steeping cured leaves of the Camellia sinensis plant in hot water, with variations based on processing methods (e.g., oxidation). Common types include black, green, white, oolong, and herbal infusions.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1-2 tsp sugar
  • 1-2 tsp loose tea leaves
  • ginger

Steps

  1. Boil water in a saucepan
  2. Add tea leaves and ginger for 2–3 minutes
  3. Pour in milk and sugar, bring to a gentle boil
  4. Simmer 1–2 more minutes, then strain into a cup
  5. Serve hot
